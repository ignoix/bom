Enos knelt and prayed all day and night in the wilderness; God's voice pronounced forgiveness. Intercession expanded from self to tribe to enemies — God covenanted to preserve the records until the last days. Enos preached his whole life and passed the plates to his son Jarom.旷野跪祷昼夜，神声赦罪；三层代祷，由己及族及敌，为拉曼立约存版末世；传道终身，交版于子雅龙。
