c. 399–361 BC (38 yrs)约公元前 399–361 年（38 年）
By keeping the law and trusting in faith, the Nephites repeatedly repelled Lamanite invasions. They smelted metals, built cities, and grew strong. Prophets preached with severity to stir the people. Jarom notes 200 years since leaving Jerusalem, then passes the gold plates to his son Omni.守律凭信，屡退拉曼入侵；冶金建城，国势日盛；先知严诫振民，记离耶路撒冷二百年之迹，金版传子奥姆乃。
