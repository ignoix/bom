Chapter 1第一章 Finding the Small Plates, Trusting to Append Them偶获小版，信托附入 Hill Cumorah · Zarahemla卡默拉山 · 柴雷罕拉 Written c. AD 385约公元后 385 年写作
While compiling the large plates, Mormon unexpectedly found the small plates. Delighted that they testify of Christ, he trusted God's mysterious purpose and appended them unchanged. He then recounts how King Benjamin expelled false prophets, repelled the Lamanites by faith, and passed the kingdom to Mosiah — opening the large-plates narrative.辑大版偶得小版，喜其彰基督之道，神旨莫测，附原版于后；溯便雅悯驱伪先知、凭信退拉曼、传位摩赛亚，启大版之端。
