c. 361–130 BC (231 yrs)约公元前 361–130 年（231 年）
Five men kept the small plates in turn, each writing little. Mosiah I was commanded to lead righteous Nephites northward, where they met the Mulekites and united at Zarahemla. Amaleki transferred the plates to King Benjamin, ending the small plates record.五人递守小版，各寡言；摩赛亚受命北迁，遇缪莱克合两族于柴雷罕拉；亚玛力以版归便雅悯，小版之记终。
